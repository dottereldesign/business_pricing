Bit Clever

No exact live result was found in Classes 35 or 42. The broader similarity tool returned many marks built around common words such as “bit” and “clever”, including “I’m Clever” and “Get Clever”, so the field is not empty.

02 / Descriptive wording

Visual Identity

No exact live result was found in Classes 35 or 42. IPONZ returned 162 similar results, including “My Identity”, “Visual Studio”, and “Visual Alchemy”. More importantly, “visual identity” directly describes a design service.

Preliminary view: it may be usable as ordinary descriptive wording, but it is a weak business name and may be difficult to register or defend as a word mark.

Before spending money

Do this
next.

A register search is only one part of clearance. Earlier use, unregistered reputation, company names, social accounts, and confusingly similar marks can still matter.

  1. 01
    Shortlist Bit Clever first.

    It has the best combination of relevant-class screening and matching domain availability.

  2. 02
    Run a broader professional search.

    Ask an IP professional to assess visual, phonetic, and conceptual similarity in the exact services you plan to sell.

  3. 03
    Check real-world use.

    Search OneCheck, the Companies Register, NZBN, Google, social platforms, and local directories before deciding.

  4. 04
    Only then buy and print.

    Secure the domain, handles, and business-name registrations before ordering cards, signage, apparel, or stationery.

Business Connect application

Prepare before
you file.

The guided form is suitable for a New Zealand individual or small business filing for itself, for a word, image, or combined word-and-image mark. It uses pre-approved goods and services terms.

Do not submit the form just to test an idea.

Finish the broader clearance check first. Filing fees are generally not refundable once a filing has been receipted and work has begun, and an application cannot usually be broadened after submission.

01 / Legal owner

File in the owner’s legal name.

  • If the owner is a sole trader, use the individual’s full legal name.
  • A trading name on its own is not a legal owner.
  • If a company should own the mark, decide and establish that structure before filing.
  • Prepare the owner’s address and a case contact with a New Zealand or Australian address.
02 / Exact mark

Choose what you are registering.

  • Words only.
  • An image or logo with no words.
  • A combined mark containing an image and words, or stylised words.
  • For an image or combined mark, prepare a JPG under 10 MB.
03 / Services and classes

Define what the mark will cover.

  • List the services the studio genuinely provides or intends to provide.
  • Build the specification from IPONZ’s pre-approved terms.
  • The selected terms determine the relevant Nice class or classes and the scope of protection.
  • Classes 35 and 42 were starting points for this screen—not an automatic filing decision.
04 / Filing admin

Leave time to review.

  • Set aside about 45 minutes and save the draft as you go.
  • The Business Connect session times out after 60 minutes of inactivity.
  • Check the application summary, invoice details, email, and every service term before paying.
  • Save the PDF, receipt, application number, filing date, and login recovery details.

What happens after payment

  1. 01
    Application filed

    IPONZ emails an application number and the filing date starts the process.

  2. 02
    Examination

    An examiner checks the legal requirements. If IPONZ sends a compliance report, respond by its deadline or request an extension before the deadline.

  3. 03
    Acceptance or refusal

    Acceptance means the application can move forward; it does not yet mean the mark is registered.

  4. 04
    Journal advertisement

    The accepted application is advertised and others have three months to oppose it.

  5. 05
    Registration wait

    Registration occurs three months after Journal publication or six months after filing, whichever is later, if no complication prevents it.

  6. 06
    Registration and maintenance

    Use the registered symbol only once registration is complete. Keep the owner and contact details current and plan for renewal every 10 years.
