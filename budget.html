Keep a separate allowance

Costs that depend
on the business.

01

GST and foreign-card fees. Totals above are before GST and use a planning exchange rate, not your card provider’s final rate.

02

Insurance. Professional indemnity and public liability pricing needs a quote based on revenue, work type, and cover.

03

Hardware and connectivity. Laptop, monitor, phone, broadband, and replacement reserves are not included.

04

Client-specific services. Domains, email, paid plugins, databases, transaction fees, stock assets, and production hosting should normally stay in the client’s name.

05

Sales and marketing. Advertising, networking, sponsorship, photography, and printed collateral remain optional growth spend.

06

Trade mark application. Use a working allowance of NZ$115 including GST per class for the first-time S&PA route: NZ$57.50 for the report plus NZ$57.50 for an unchanged application filed within three months. Two classes would total NZ$230. The lean guided route is NZ$80.50 per class; professional advice, disputes, and renewal are separate.

Software prices checked 12 Aug 2026 from linked official provider pages; the trade mark fee was checked 17 Aug 2026 against IPONZ. USD conversions use NZ$1.75/US$1 as a budgeting rate; the RBNZ B1 reference available during review showed NZD/USD around 0.577. Prices, included usage, tax treatment, and promotions can change—confirm before purchase.
