Tier two / Attach to web projects

Raise value.
Solve the whole job.

These are profitable when scoped tightly. Sell them as practical systems, not as world-changing brand consultancy.

Identity starter

Logo + mini brand kit

A usable identity direction: primary mark, simple variations, colour, type, favicon and a concise usage sheet.

  • Good for: new small businesses
  • Boundary: 2–3 directions, fixed revisions
  • Partner for: deep strategy or complex illustration
Sales collateral

Brochure + capability pack

Print-ready or digital brochures, service sheets, proposal covers and PDFs that reuse the web identity.

  • Tri-fold and multi-page brochures
  • Case-study one-pagers
  • Capability statement
  • Print-ready export and handoff
Campaign kit

Social + ad templates

A finite set of editable campaign templates rather than an endless “social media management” commitment.

  • Figma or Canva-ready masters
  • Platform crops
  • Launch graphics
  • Simple usage guide
Lead generation

Lead magnet system

A useful PDF, checklist, calculator or quiz plus landing page, delivery email and measurement.

Communication

Email template kit

Branded email signatures, newsletter layout, automated response templates and basic campaign setup.

Practical automation

Workflow setup

Connect forms, spreadsheets, inboxes and simple CRM stages. Document the workflow so the client owns it.

Boundaries make you credible

Do not bluff
the specialist work.

You can coordinate a broader outcome while being transparent about who owns the specialist judgement.

Partner or refer

Bring in a specialist

  • Complex brand strategy and naming clearance
  • High-end custom illustration or 3D
  • Photography and video production
  • Long-form conversion copywriting
  • Large paid-media management
  • Penetration testing and regulated security
  • Legal, privacy and tax advice
Avoid for now

High-risk distractions

  • 24/7 emergency support with no coverage plan
  • Unlimited revisions or “unlimited design”
  • Guaranteed SEO rankings or revenue
  • Large custom platforms at brochure-site prices
  • Full social management without content capacity
  • Owning client domains, ads or critical accounts
  • Cheap logos with unlimited concepts
Real talk

Your edge is not claiming every discipline. It is being the technically capable, design-aware person who can see the whole web job, communicate clearly and bring in help before quality drops.
